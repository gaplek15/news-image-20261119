# THE WORLD — Thursday, November 19, 2026

A single broadsheet page summing up Thursday, 19 November 2026 in one
image, built for make.fun's news-image bounty ($250 in USDC, top 50
entries share the prize).

## What's on the page

- **Trump, Xi and Putin hold first-ever talks** — the first three-way
  meeting of the US, Chinese and Russian leaders, 80 minutes at the
  APEC summit in Shenzhen: nuclear dialogue, fuel supply, UN reform.
- **Myanmar committed genocide, ICJ rules** — the world court finds
  Myanmar guilty over the Rohingya in 2017, 9–6.
- **Cyclone Akvan nears India's east coast** — 72,000 evacuate in
  Andhra Pradesh and Odisha; landfall due at noon Friday.
- **Nvidia posts a record $115.8B quarter** — revenue more than doubles
  on AI demand; the board rules out Jensen Huang's return as CEO.
- **World in Brief** — India–Japan 2+2 talks; the Prince and Princess
  of Wales meet PM Modi in Delhi; Kerch Bridge hit again; Russian
  strikes kill five; France sends troops to Ukraine; Takaichi meets
  Putin.

Every story carries its own bold meme-style cartoon illustration.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The script copies the bundled `news.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, unchanged. No network, no clock,
no randomness — the same output every time.
